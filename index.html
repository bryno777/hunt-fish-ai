PHASE 2D • MAP-BASED SPOT PLANNER

Choose a species and fishing style. The app ranks waters using species, water size, access, live weather, and your selected method. Phase 2D adds a map-based spot planner that turns the species, method, weather and structure plan into approximate target zones. FIND A WATER NEAR YOU

Use your phone's GPS to rank the mapped waters by straight-line distance. This does not determine legal access; verify current access and regulations in NGPC's interactive maps.

PHASE 1 DATA SOURCES

This build uses Nebraska Game & Parks Commission's 2026 Fishing Guide as the first structured dataset. NGPC says its public-water resources include water-body size, fish species and regulations. Its lake-contour system covers 125+ mapped waters and includes underwater habitat/structure information.
