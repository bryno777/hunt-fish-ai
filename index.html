🎯 PHASE 2F • HABITAT + STRUCTURE INTELLIGENCE
PHASE 2E • LIVE NGPC BATHYMETRY

Pick a species and method, then choose a mapped water. The planner draws approximate target zones around the verified water coordinate using species behavior and structure patterns. These are not surveyed GPS fishing spots.

🌲 Phase 2F structure intelligence

Select a water and species, then rank the structure patterns that deserve attention first. Where NGPC has a published habitat/structure project, the app flags that official resource for verification.

⭐ PHASE 2H • AI SPOT RANKING

Combines the selected species, fishing method, official NGPC contour depth, available habitat-map flag, and current weather/time window to rank the best mapped contour targets. This is a decision aid—not a claim that fish are present at a point.
